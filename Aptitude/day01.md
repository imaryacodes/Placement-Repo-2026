#GEOMETRIC PROGRESSION

sequence of non-zero numbers, ratio of any term with its preceding term is always constant.

Form - x, xr, xr3, xr4...
first term = a
- nth term = xr^(n -1)
- Sum of n terms = x(1 – r^n) / (1-r), here r < 1
- Sum of n terms = x(r^n - 1) / (r-1), here r > 1



#ARITHMETIC PROGRESSION

sequence of numbers with each term after first is found by adding a constant to preceding term

Form - x, x+y, x+2y, x+3y....
- nth term = x+(n-1)y
- Sum of n terms = n/2 * [2x+(n-1)y]
- 1+2+3+......+n = [n(n+1)] / 2
- 1+2^2+3^2+.......+n^2 = [n(n+1)(2n+1)] / 6
- for sum of cubes = {[n(n+1)] / 2} ^ 2

#DIVISIBILITY

- By 2 : units digit has 0,2,4,6,8
- By 3 : sum of all digits is divisible by 3
- By 4 : last two digits divisible by 4
- By 5 : unit digit is 0 or 5
- By 6 : numbers divisble by 2 and 3 both
- By 11 : difference between sum of digits at even place and sum of digits at odd place must be 0 or 11
- By 12 : numbers divisible by 4 and 3



#IMPORTANT
- To find a number, say b is divisible by a, find two numbers m and n, such that m*n = a, where m and n are co-prime numbers and if b is divisible by both m and n then it is divisible by a.
- Sum of the first n odd numbers = n2
- Sum of first n even numbers = n ( n + 1)
- Dividend = [(Divisor × Quotient)] + Remainde
- If Dividend = a^n + b^n or a^n – b^n
    = if n is even : a^n - b^n is divisible by (a + b)
    = if n is odd : a^n + b^n is divisible by (a - b)
    = a^n - b^n is always divisible by (a – b)
- To find the unit digit of number in form : a^b
    = If b is not divisible by 4, then Units digit is a^r, r is the remainder.
    = If b is multiple of 4,
        ~ units digit is 6 (when even nums raised to multiple of 4)
        ~ units digit is 1 (when odd nums raised to multiple of 4)
