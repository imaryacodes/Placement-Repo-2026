# SIMPLE & COMPOUND INTEREST

1. PRINCIPAL - The money borrowed or lent out for a certain period

2. INTEREST - Extra money paid for using other's money

3. SIMPLE INTEREST
- If the interest on a sum borrowed for certain period is reckoned uniformly.
- Formula = (P*R*T)/100

4. ANNUAL COMPOUND INTEREST
Amount = P*(1+R/100)^n
Amount = P*(1+R/100)^n - P

5. HALF-YEARLY COMPOUND INTEREST
Amount = P*(1+(R/2)/100)^2n

6. QUARTERLY COMPOUND INTEREST
Amount = P*(1+(R/4)/100)^4n

7. ANNUAL COMPOUND INTEREST, FRACTIONAL TIME
Amount = P * (1+R/100)^3 * (1+(frac*R)/100)

8. DIFFERENT RATES 
Amount = P * (1+R1/100) * (1+R2/100) * (1+R3/100)...

9. PRESENT WORTH OF Rs.X due 'n' YEARS
Present worth = X/(1+R/100)^n
